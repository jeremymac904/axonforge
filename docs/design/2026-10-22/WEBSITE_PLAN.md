# Axon Forge Labs website direction

October 22, 2026. Planning summary accompanying the selected visual design.

## Purpose

Make Axon Forge Labs the public home for Jeremy McDonald's software, AI systems, creative tools, and practical education. Help visitors understand the company, discover its platforms, learn useful workflows, and contact the team.

Selected hero: **AI that gets real work done.**

Proposed support copy: Software. Agent systems. Creative tools. Practical education.

## Homepage

1. Company promise, approved AF visual, Explore Platforms and Start Learning actions.
2. Selected platform cards with accurate availability and working destinations.
3. The AI Briefing: original AI news and practical analysis with real sources and dates.
4. Tutorials: useful learning from Jeremy's existing sites, preserving source credit and destinations.
5. Founder introduction and a full biography link.
6. Clearly labeled AI collaborators directed by Jeremy, with accurate roles.
7. Work With Us contact section and complete support/policy footer.

Product screenshots and article titles in the image are illustrative. Feature actual platforms and verified content when implementing. Do not turn an internal build or a roadmap entry into a public availability claim.

## Navigation and pages

Main navigation: Platforms, AI News, Tutorials, The Lab, About. Primary contact action: Work With Us.

Preserve existing app/support URLs. Plan compatibility or redirects for overlapping Apps, Products and Portfolio routes before a release.

## Founder biography

Use Jeremy's experience in mortgages and finance since 2003, team leadership, AI training, entrepreneurship, and practical software building. A longer biography can include his football/coaching background, fitness interests, travel and bass guitar where relevant. Use approved real identity assets and verified current role descriptions.

Candidate first-person introduction:

> I'm Jeremy McDonald, founder of Axon Forge Labs, mortgage broker, team leader, and AI trainer. I've worked in mortgages and finance since 2003, helping people understand complicated decisions and find practical solutions. That same approach drives my work with AI: build something useful, test it in the real world, and teach people how to use it.

This is draft website copy. Do not invent credentials, performance claims, company ownership relationships, or customer testimonials. Axon Forge Labs should not be presented as owning third-party professional organizations.

## News and tutorials

Daily AI news is the editorial target. Retain the current manual authoring/publication direction; saving this plan does not activate a publishing automation. Articles should distinguish announcement dates from publication dates, cite original sources, explain practical use and actual limitations, and use real author/reviewer records.

Tutorial entries should lead readers to the appropriate existing site, course, or video. Use original summaries, source attribution, clear prerequisites and useful outcomes. Do not duplicate entire courses or imply a lesson outline is a completed video.

## Technical continuity

The existing repository uses Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind, npm, static export to `out/`, and Netlify configuration. Preserve the existing stack and working code unless a future authorized build establishes a reason to change them. No backend, CMS, framework migration, publishing schedule, domain change, or deployment is part of this design save.
